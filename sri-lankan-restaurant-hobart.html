New to Sri Lankan food?

What should I try?

Start with Rice & Curry

A great introduction to the variety of flavours, vegetables, sambols and curries found in Sri Lankan cooking.

Try Kottu

A popular Sri Lankan street-food favourite made for a hearty, flavour-packed meal. Check our menu for current options.

Experience Hoppers

Crisp-edged, soft-centred hoppers are one of Sri Lanka's distinctive favourites. Availability can vary, so contact us before travelling specifically for hoppers.
