Our Food

Our menu brings together the food we love from Sri Lanka and the familiar café favourites our local community enjoys. Some dishes rotate with the day, season and availability, so there is often something different to discover.

Think fragrant rice, colourful curries, crisp hoppers, chopped kottu and freshly prepared short eats — food with texture, spice and plenty of character. Call us if you would like to know what is cooking today.

The Sri Lankan table

One meal, many flavours

A Sri Lankan rice and curry meal is rarely about one curry alone. It is the combination that makes it special: rice with vegetables, dhal, sambols, pickles and a meat or seafood curry, each bringing a different flavour and texture to the plate.

Our daily combinations and specials vary, allowing us to keep the menu interesting and make the most of what is available.
