Privacy Notice

This notice explains how 52 South Cafe & Restaurant uses information submitted through this website.

Information we collect

When you request a table or contact us, we may receive your name, phone number, email address, booking details, dietary requirements and any comments you provide.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to you, manage your booking, provide restaurant services, prevent misuse of our forms and maintain necessary business records.

Booking processing

When you submit a table request, FormSubmit processes the information so booking notifications can be delivered to authorised 52 South business inboxes and an acknowledgement can be sent to you. Do not include payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information in the notes field.

Analytics and advertising cookies

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand website visits and actions such as menu views, booking requests, phone calls and online-ordering clicks. We may also use the Meta Pixel for advertising measurement. These optional services are not loaded unless you select “Accept analytics & ads”. TikTok measurement will remain disabled until a business-owned TikTok Pixel is configured, and it will use the same consent choice if enabled.

Cookie choices

You can continue with essential website features without optional analytics or advertising cookies. Use the “Cookie settings” button in the website footer at any time to review or change your choice.

Maps and directions

Our Contact page includes a Google map, and direction links open Google Maps. Google may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details and interaction with the map under Google's own privacy terms.

Sharing, access and retention

We do not sell your personal information. Booking information is available only to authorised 52 South personnel and service providers needed to operate the website and booking process, or where required by law. We retain booking records only for as long as reasonably needed to manage reservations, respond to enquiries and maintain necessary business records.

Your choices

You can request access to or correction of information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it when we no longer need it.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact 52 South Cafe & Restaurant at 52south.au@gmail.com or call 0492 144 209.
